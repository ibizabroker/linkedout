Privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026

LinkedOut is a Chrome extension that filters and tracks LinkedIn job listings. It has no servers of its own. Everything it keeps stays in your browser or in your own Google Drive.

What LinkedOut reads

  • LinkedIn job pages. On www.linkedin.com, LinkedOut reads job cards and job details so it can hide jobs you chose to hide, add Block buttons, and record jobs you apply to.
  • LinkedIn job facts. To check whether a job was reposted, how many people applied, and the details the tracker records, LinkedOut asks LinkedIn for them from your browser, using your own LinkedIn session. Nothing goes through a third party.
  • The current tab's address, when you open the LinkedOut popup, to tell whether you are on a LinkedIn Jobs page.

What LinkedOut stores on your device

Your settings (which jobs to hide and the applicant limit), the companies you blocked, a short-lived cache of job facts, and, when the tracker is connected, the sheet's ID and a queue of rows waiting to be written. This lives in Chrome's extension storage on your device. Uninstalling LinkedOut deletes it.

Google Sheets job tracker (optional)

If you connect the tracker, you sign in with Google through Chrome, and LinkedOut creates a spreadsheet called "LinkedOut Job Tracker" in your Google Drive. When you apply to a job, it adds a row with: applied on, company, job title, status, location, workplace, applied via, applicants when applied, reposted, last update, notes, job link and job ID.

LinkedOut keeps a local copy of the application dates from your sheet on your device, to show your stats.

LinkedOut asks for one Google permission, drive.file. It can only see and change files that LinkedOut itself created. It cannot read anything else in your Drive. Chrome keeps the sign-in token; LinkedOut uses it only to write to that spreadsheet.

LinkedOut's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What LinkedOut does not do

  • It does not send your data to the developer or to any server other than LinkedIn and Google, as described above.
  • It has no analytics, tracking, or ads.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer your data, and does not use it for anything unrelated to the features above.

Your choices

  • Disconnect in LinkedOut Settings → Account revokes LinkedOut's Google access and clears the tracker's local data. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The spreadsheet is yours. Delete it from Google Drive whenever you like.
  • Uninstalling LinkedOut removes everything it stored on your device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/ibizabroker/linkedout.

LinkedOut is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.
