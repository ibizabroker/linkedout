# Homepage (site/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: job seekers who use LinkedIn Jobs daily and are tired of re-seeing applied, viewed, reposted and agency jobs. Action: Add to Chrome (Chrome Web Store). Proof: the real extension, running live on the page against fictional jobs. Constraints: no third-party requests; follows system light/dark; full brief in docs/homepage-brief.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page proves the product by letting the visitor clear the noise themselves, and narrates the result in one ledger line. It refuses the category default of a headline, a screenshot and a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Ledger. Warm paper (#FDFCFA / night #1E1D1B), ink and pencil neutrals, hairline rules, LinkedIn blue only for what is on and the fix, ink keys with a heavier bottom edge, Schibsted Grotesk (self-hosted) for display, the system UI font for everything else.

STORY: The visitor sees a noisy job list, flips a switch, watches cards fold away while the tally line strikes through what was cleared, blocks an agency, applies to a job and sees the row land in the sheet, then clicks Add to Chrome.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with logo, Privacy and GitHub. Headline "LinkedIn Jobs, without the noise." at about 76px on the left; Add to Chrome key with "Desktop Chrome extension" beneath on the right of the same band. Under a hairline, the live tally line in 22px display type (jobs on the page, struck-through already-seen count, agencies, worth your time in blue), between hairlines. Below, the browser frame spanning the content width with tabs, address bar, toolbar icon, list and details, the real popup open under the toolbar icon. The frame's top half is above the fold at 1440x900.

FORM: Narrated ledger, dealt lead (index 5 of 7 grounded structures). seed:f29a5cd975e223fb. Raise from design-canon-creator-hardware-bench: every switch and key on the page travels when pressed. Signature interaction: the tally line rewrites and strikes through live as the demo changes state; cards fold out and back.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
