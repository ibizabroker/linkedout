<p align="center">LinkedIn Jobs, without the noise.</p>

LinkedOut is a browser extension for job seekers. It removes the job cards you don't need to see again from LinkedIn's Jobs results, and it can keep a log of every job you apply to.

## Features

- Hide jobs you've applied to, viewed or saved, and jobs that were reposted. Each one is a switch in the popup.
- Set an applicant limit, and LinkedOut hides jobs with more applicants than that.
- Click Block on any job card, or in the job pane to stop seeing that company. Manage the list in Settings. Really helpful to block the annoying staffing agencies.
- Optional feature to track jobs in your Google Sheets, each job you apply to is added as a row in a sheet in your own Drive. You can update the status anytime you want.

## Install

LinkedOut is currently available in [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/fpbfombjfoakhodoebmgnmfjjeflmpbi). Firefox support is planned for the future release.

## Privacy

LinkedOut has no servers, analytics or ads. Your settings stay in your browser, and the tracker's sheet lives in your Google Drive. Read the full [privacy policy](https://ibizabroker.github.io/linkedout/privacy/).
